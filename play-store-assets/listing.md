# Google Play listing text

App name (30 max): ClockShift

## Short description (80 max)

Check in, check out, and always know your overtime balance. No account, no ads.

## Full description (4000 max)

ClockShift is a simple work time tracker that keeps count of your overtime, so you never have to wonder how many hours you are ahead or behind.

Check in when you start, check out when you finish, and ClockShift does the maths. Forget to log a day? Your usual hours are assumed, so you only record the days that were different.

CHECK IN AND OUT
• One tap to start your day and one to finish it
• Live timer with your progress towards today's target and your expected finish time
• Start and end breaks as you go
• Forgot to check out? ClockShift reminds you and makes it quick to set the right end time

STANDARD DAYS, NO FUSS
• Set your working days and usual hours once: start, end and break
• Days you don't log count as a standard day, so your balance stays correct without daily effort
• Customise individual weekdays if your hours vary
• Change your schedule later and past days keep the hours they had

FIX ANY DAY
• Add, edit or delete work blocks and breaks on any past day
• Mark vacation, holidays or sick days as days off
• Work on a day off or a weekend counts entirely as overtime
• Swipe actions and undo make corrections painless

OVERTIME THAT ADDS UP
• Your balance, always visible on the home screen
• Overview for all time, this week, this month, last month, this year or any custom range
• Bar chart of overtime per day, week or month
• Breakdown showing worked time against target for each period
• Already have a flex balance? Enter your starting balance and start date

EVENING REMINDER
• An optional notification after work asks how your day went
• Log your standard day straight from the notification, or choose to customise it
• Choose the time, or turn it off completely

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Everything stays on your device
• No account, no sign-in
• No ads, no analytics, no tracking
• The app doesn't even request Internet access
• Open source: github.com/guidohu/clockshift

DESIGNED FOR ANDROID
• Material 3 design with dynamic colour on Android 12 and newer
• Light and dark themes
• Follows your system's 12 or 24-hour clock and your locale's first day of the week

Whether you use flexible hours, a fixed schedule or somewhere in between, ClockShift keeps the balance honest and gets out of your way.
