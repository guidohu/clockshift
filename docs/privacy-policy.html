Privacy Policy — ClockShift

Last updated: November 10, 2026

ClockShift does not collect personal data, and nothing you enter ever leaves your device because of the app.

ClockShift is a work time tracker published by the developer (GitHub: guidohu). This policy explains what the app stores, what it does with it, and the one way your data can leave your phone without the app sending it.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your work schedule: working days, usual start and end times, and break lengths.
  • Your time entries: check-in and check-out times, breaks, and any notes you write.
  • Days you marked as days off.
  • Your settings: tracking start date, starting overtime balance, and reminder preferences.
  • If you use automatic check-in (ClockShift Pro): the names of your office Wi‑Fi networks, or the coordinates and radius of your workplace, and when your phone last arrived at or left work.

All of this is stored locally on your device only, in a private database that other apps cannot read. The developer has no access to it.

What the app does not do

  • It does not request the Internet permission and makes no network requests of any kind.
  • It has no accounts, sign-in, or user profile.
  • It contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or third-party tracking code.
  • It does not access your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or files. It uses your location only for the optional automatic check-in described below, and never sends it anywhere.
  • It does not share, sell, or transfer your data to anyone.

Permissions the app uses

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): to show the optional reminders and automatic check-in notices. They are generated on your device. You can switch each one off in the app or deny the permission, and the rest of the app keeps working.
  • Receive boot completed (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): so reminders and automatic check-in are set up again after your device restarts, the app is updated, or the time or time zone changes.
  • Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION, ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION): only for the optional automatic check-in, as described above. It is requested only when you set that feature up.
  • Network and Wi‑Fi state (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, ACCESS_WIFI_STATE): to notice when your phone joins a Wi‑Fi network, for automatic check-in by office Wi‑Fi. These do not allow the app to go online.
  • Wake lock and foreground service (WAKE_LOCK, FOREGROUND_SERVICE): added by Android's WorkManager library, which runs the optional daily backup reliably in the background.
  • Google Play billing (com.android.vending.BILLING): to offer the optional ClockShift Pro subscription through the Play Store app.

Automatic check-in (optional, ClockShift Pro)

If you turn on automatic check-in, ClockShift notices when you arrive at and leave work so it can check you in and out. It works in one of two ways, which you choose:

  • Office Wi‑Fi: the app compares the name of the Wi‑Fi network your phone is connected to with the names you entered. Android only reveals network names to apps with location permission, which is why this mode asks for it.
  • Location: the app asks Google Play services on your device to report when your phone enters or leaves an area around your workplace (a geofence). How Google Play services determines location is governed by your device's location settings and Google's privacy policy.

To work while the app is closed, automatic check-in needs location access "all the time". The decision to check you in or out is made on your device, and the only things stored are those listed above. Nothing about your location or networks is sent to the developer or anyone else. Turning automatic check-in off, or denying the permission, stops it; the rest of the app keeps working.

Exports and backups

You can export a timesheet (PDF or CSV) and save a backup of all your data. These files are only created when you ask for them, and they go only where you send them: a location you pick with Android's file picker, or the app you choose in the share sheet. The optional daily backup (ClockShift Pro) writes to a folder or file you chose the same way, which may belong to a storage app such as Nextcloud, ownCloud or Google Drive. What that app does with the file is governed by its own terms; ClockShift itself does not upload anything. Restoring a backup replaces the data in the app with the data from the file you pick.

Purchases

ClockShift Pro is a subscription sold through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google; the app only learns from the Play Store app on your device whether a subscription is active. The developer does not receive your payment details. Purchases are governed by Google's privacy policy and the Google Play terms.

Android backup

ClockShift allows Android's standard backup and device-transfer features to include its local database. If you have Google backup turned on for your device, Android may store a copy of the app's data in your own Google account (for example, in Google Drive backup), and may copy it to a new device when you set one up. This is a feature of your operating system and is governed by your Google account settings and Google's privacy policy. The developer does not receive this data and cannot access it. You can turn this off in your device's system settings.

Deleting your data

You are in control of your data. You can edit or delete any entry inside the app. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all of its data from your device. Backup copies kept by Android (see above) are managed in your Google account settings.

Children

ClockShift is not directed at children and, because it collects no personal data from anyone, it does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, this policy will be updated before the change is released, and the "Last updated" date above will change.

Contact

This app is open source. You can review exactly what it does at github.com/guidohu/clockshift. Questions about this policy can be raised via GitHub Issues.
