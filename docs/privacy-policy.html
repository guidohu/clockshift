Privacy Policy — ClockShift

Last updated: October 8, 2026

ClockShift does not collect personal data, and nothing you enter ever leaves your device because of the app.

ClockShift is a work time tracker published by the developer (GitHub: guidohu). This policy explains what the app stores, what it does with it, and the one way your data can leave your phone without the app sending it.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your work schedule: working days, usual start and end times, and break lengths.
  • Your time entries: check-in and check-out times, breaks, and any notes you write.
  • Days you marked as days off.
  • Your settings: tracking start date, starting overtime balance, and reminder preferences.

All of this is stored locally on your device only, in a private database that other apps cannot read. The developer has no access to it.

What the app does not do

  • It does not request the Internet permission and makes no network requests of any kind.
  • It has no accounts, sign-in, or user profile.
  • It contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or third-party tracking code.
  • It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or files.
  • It does not share, sell, or transfer your data to anyone.

Permissions the app uses

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): to show the optional daily reminder. The reminder is generated on your device. You can switch it off in the app or deny the permission, and the rest of the app keeps working.
  • Receive boot completed (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): so the reminder is scheduled again after your device restarts, the app is updated, or the time or time zone changes.

Android backup

ClockShift allows Android's standard backup and device-transfer features to include its local database. If you have Google backup turned on for your device, Android may store a copy of the app's data in your own Google account (for example, in Google Drive backup), and may copy it to a new device when you set one up. This is a feature of your operating system and is governed by your Google account settings and Google's privacy policy. The developer does not receive this data and cannot access it. You can turn this off in your device's system settings.

Deleting your data

You are in control of your data. You can edit or delete any entry inside the app. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all of its data from your device. Backup copies kept by Android (see above) are managed in your Google account settings.

Children

ClockShift is not directed at children and, because it collects no personal data from anyone, it does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, this policy will be updated before the change is released, and the "Last updated" date above will change.

Contact

This app is open source. You can review exactly what it does at github.com/guidohu/clockshift. Questions about this policy can be raised via GitHub Issues.
